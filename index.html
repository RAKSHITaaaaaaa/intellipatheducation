QUESTIONS

Parent questions, answered

Still unsure? Email info@intellipatheducation.com and we will reply within one business day.

Which subjects do you teach?

Maths and Science for Years 1–12. English is opening soon — you can join the waitlist on this page and we will email you first.

What year levels do you cover?

Years 1 to 12, from primary foundations through to senior courses and the ATAR pathway.

How do the lessons run?

Live online sessions, one-on-one or in a small group, scheduled around Australian time zones.

What happens in the free trial?

It is a friendly first session where we meet your child. That first session is diagnostic: we find exactly where they stand, then build their lesson plan around it.

Do you follow the Australian Curriculum?

Yes. We teach to what your child’s school teaches, Foundation to Year 10 and the senior and ATAR pathway. Tell us your state and year level and we match the syllabus.

Do you help with NAPLAN and Selective tests?

Yes. Timed practice papers, worked solutions and exam technique for NAPLAN, Selective and OC, plus competition exams.

How much does it cost?

Plans depend on year level, subject and whether you choose one-on-one or a small group. Email info@intellipatheducation.com or message us on WhatsApp and we will send you current pricing.

How do you use my details?

Only to contact you about IntelliPath lessons and the free resources you ask for. We do not sell or share them.
